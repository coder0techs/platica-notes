Recording

Every new meeting starts here. The in-meeting language pill overrides just that meeting and is never saved back, so a one-off switch cannot leak into the next call.

A meeting is recording right now. This change applies to the next one. To switch the live meeting, use its in-meeting language pill.

Languages you switch between

Pick up to three. Each one gets its own button in the meeting, so switching is a single click instead of a trip through the dropdown. Nothing is hidden: the full list stays in the language pill for the meeting you did not see coming.

Where files go

Keep this folder out of your cloud sync.

Paths are relative to your Downloads folder, the only place a browser extension may write. Nested paths like meetings/work are fine, and files are filed by month inside them.

Meeting history

Once the list is full, the oldest entry drops off. The .md files already in your Downloads folder are never touched. What you lose is the ability to download that meeting again from this extension.

Troubleshooting

During a meeting, Alt+Shift+B drops a bookmark into the transcript at that moment, and Alt+Shift+H hides every on-screen control without stopping the recording.
