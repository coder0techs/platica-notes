Plática Notes — Settings

Recording

New meetings start in this language. You can switch it just for the current meeting from the in-meeting language pill; that choice is not saved.

Private meetings download to the Private folder below — keep that folder out of your cloud sync.

During a meeting, press Alt+Shift+B to drop a bookmark into the transcript at that moment.

Folders

Folders are relative to your Downloads folder; nested paths like "meetings/work" are allowed.

Advanced

Off by default. Saved to the Debug-log folder above — keep it out of your cloud sync (it contains the full transcript). Private meetings are never logged.
