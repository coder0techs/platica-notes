Plática Notes records your meetings on your own computer

Google Meet's own live captions and in-meeting chat, saved as one Markdown file per call. No servers, no accounts, no network requests: the extension has nowhere to send anything, by design.

  1. Join a Google Meet call

    Capture starts by itself. A small control group appears at the top of the call showing that it is recording, and in which language.

  2. Talk, and watch the transcript if you want to

    The live panel shows speech and chat on one timeline. You can add your own notes, mark a moment, mark the call private, or pause capture at any point.

  3. Leave, and find the file in your Downloads folder

    One .md per meeting, filed by month. Deliberately no summary: you get the raw material and hand it to whichever assistant you already use, with your own prompt. Nothing leaves your machine until you send it.
